AI is becoming the interface to everything.

Not just search. Not just content. The answers you get, the options presented to you, the decisions you make – all increasingly flow through AI systems shaped by the incentives of the platforms that control them. Ads are showing up. Responses are being influenced. And unlike the old internet, you can't view source to see what's happening.

Right now, users have almost no way to see when something is being influenced, understand how outputs are being shaped, or set boundaries on what they will and won't accept.

Adblocked AI is a user-side layer that starts with what's obvious – ads – and builds toward giving you visibility and control over the rest.

How It Works

  1. 01

    Install the Extension

    One click from the Chrome Web Store. adblocked.ai runs locally in your browser with zero configuration.

  2. 02

    Use AI Normally

    Open ChatGPT and chat as usual. The extension works silently in the background. More platforms coming.

  3. 03

    See the Difference

    Ads and promoted content are stripped before they reach you. Your conversation is yours again. This is the first layer – more visibility is coming.

Design Philosophy
Behind Adblocked AI

No Data Collection

adblocked.ai runs entirely on your device. Your conversations never leave your browser. We can't see what you say even if we wanted to.

Open Source

The code is public. Every decision the extension makes is auditable. A tool that claims to surface hidden influence cannot itself be a black box.

Privacy by Design

No analytics, no tracking, no server-side anything. Built around GDPR principles as a design choice, not a compliance checkbox.
